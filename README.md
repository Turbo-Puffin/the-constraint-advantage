## What this draft is — and isn't

- **The spine:** simplicity + constraints → peace → success. Underneath it, the whole book is a love story for Matt's wife and kids, fueled by regret over missed time. Lighter than heavy, wittier than drab, profanity roughly once per chapter at the peak.
- **True things stay true.** First-person career material is Matt's verified history (Cantina/agency years, "burnout by a thousand cuts" from his 2015 Medium essay, City of Boston / boston.gov / the 2016 open-sourcing, the union 7-hour day, the solo-operator-with-agents receipt, the ongoing house purge, the photograph-it-and-the-agent-sells-it example). Vivid scenes that are *not* from his life run in second person, so nothing about him is invented.
- **`[STORY SLOT]` brackets** mark where Matt's real anecdotes go — the chapter 1 regret moment is the most important one in the book and is still a stand-in. Nothing with Megan or the kids is fabricated; those slots wait for him. Family appears only as "my wife" / "my kids," never by name, per standing rule.
- Word count ≈ 26,000 (draft 1 runs lean by design — chapters expand where Matt's stories land).

## How to review

Read in order — the chapters call back to each other constantly (gates, the front row, the seventh minute, the watchers). React anywhere, at any altitude: a chapter that's wrong, a paragraph that doesn't sound like you, a word you'd never use. Send notes on Discord, or edit directly and commit — whatever's fastest. Every `[STORY SLOT]` you fill makes the next pass better.
