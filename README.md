# The Constraint Advantage
### Simplify to Find Peace (working subtitle) — Matthew Crist

**Status: fresh start at outline v5, 2026-07-04.** Matt's call, same night the reframe shipped: "All previous outlines and drafts are deleted in my mind. We're starting at v5." The manuscript is being written fresh against [`outline.md`](outline.md) — nothing in `manuscript/` predates v5.

## The book

Your life is cluttered in every dimension, not just the garage — the calendar, the phone, the head, the group text, the budget. Clutter is chaos on a payment plan: every cluttered domain bills you a little, forever. Constraint is the decision that stops the billing. **Peace is what's left.** Underneath it, the whole book is a love story for Matt's wife and kids, fueled by regret over missed time. Success shows up as a receipt, not the destination.

**19 chapters + close, ~50–60k words at full weight.** Part I — The Chaos · Part II — The Purge · Part III — The Quiet House.

## Rules the draft is written under

- First person = Matt's verified history only. Vivid scenes that aren't from his life run in second person. Nothing about him is invented.
- `[STORY SLOT]` brackets mark where his real anecdotes go — the chapter 1 regret moment is the most important one in the book and still waits on him. Family appears only as "my wife" / "my kids," never by name.
- Voice = the locked signature v2 (2015–18 essays canonical). Lighter than heavy, wittier than drab, profanity roughly once per chapter at the peak.
- No invented stats. The "300,000 objects" figure is out; "a lot" does the job.
