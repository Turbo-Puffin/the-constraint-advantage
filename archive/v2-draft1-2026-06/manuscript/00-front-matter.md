### A note before we start.

Everything in this book is true, which I mention because the genre has a problem. Books like this are usually written by someone who optimized his life into a spreadsheet at 31 and would like to sell you the spreadsheet. I'm not that guy. I went too hard for too long, missed things I don't get back, and figured out the fix embarrassingly late. This book is the bill, itemized, so you don't have to pay it yourself.
